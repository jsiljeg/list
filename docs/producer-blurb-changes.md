# Producer blurb changes — 2026-07-27

Every producer description changed in the session of 27 July 2026, with the text
before and after. The same change was made in **all eight languages**
(hr, en, it, fr, de, zh, sl, es); English and Croatian are reproduced here for
checking. Source of truth is `data/producers.json`.

## Why these 17

A sweep of the **79 producers with more than one wine on the list** looked for
blurbs that only accounted for part of what a guest can order. Thirteen were
rewritten in that pass; four more (Rémi Jobard, Magliano, Isole e Olena,
Poggio Scalette) had already been fixed earlier the same day for the same
reason.

**One was factually wrong:** Matošević was described as the modern face of
Istrian Malvazija, but the only Matošević wines on the list are the two Grimalda
blends — no Malvazija at all.

**One was factually loose:** Montevertine's "pure Sangiovese, no cabernet mask"
is true of Le Pergole Torte, but the estate wine carries Canaiolo and Colorino.

**One error of mine, later corrected:** the first Kunjas rewrite called Pagadebit
a nearly extinct variety. It is not a variety — it is Korčula's local name for
Plavac mali, which `wines.json` had right all along.

## Claims worth verifying

These came from research, not from the wine list, and should be checked before
they are treated as house facts:

- Ridge Geyserville as a benchmark **since 1966**
- **Vittorio Fiore's** consulting career, the Ruffoli hill, and his son **Jurij**
- **Grk** surviving essentially only in the sands of **Lumbarda**
- the **Pagadebit** etymology ("pays the debts")
- **Montevertine** having left the Chianti Classico DOCG

**EN from:** The modern face of Istrian Malvazija — fresh, precise, sun-lit whites that redefined the grape.

**EN to:** Ivica Matošević rewrote what Istrian white could be, then went inland to Grimalda — a cooler hillside of clay and flysch, where his red and white blends find the shape and shade the coast cannot give.

**HR from:** Moderno lice istarske malvazije — svježa, precizna i sunčana bijela vina koja su redefinirala sortu.

**HR to:** Ivica Matošević promijenio je predodžbu o istarskom bijelom vinu, a onda je otišao u unutrašnjost, u Grimaldu — hladniji brijeg gline i fliša, gdje njegove crvene i bijele kupaže dobivaju oblik i sjenu kakvu obala ne može dati.

## 2. Kunjas

**EN from:** Pošip from Dalmatian stone — a white of salt and almond, simple and honest as a fisherman's table.

**EN to:** Korčula in three voices — Pošip with the salt still on it, Grk from the sands of Lumbarda that grows almost nowhere else on earth, and a Plavac the island still calls Pagadebit, “the debt-payer”, from the days when a good vintage settled the year.

**HR from:** Pošip iz dalmatinskog kamena — bijelo vino soli i badema, jednostavno i pošteno kao ribarski stol.

**HR to:** Korčula u tri glasa — pošip na kojem se još osjeti sol, grk iz lumbarajskih pijesaka kakav gotovo nigdje drugdje ne raste, i plavac koji otok još zove pagadebit, „onaj koji plaća dugove“, iz vremena kad je dobra berba podmirivala godinu.
