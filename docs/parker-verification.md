# Parker ratings — verification state

Owner has a robertparker.com subscription. Method, URL pattern and the
non-vintage rule are in CLAUDE.md, section "Verifying a Parker score".
Needs the Claude Chrome extension connected and the browser logged in.

## Done — all of them

**74 of 74 Parker ratings carry `checked`** as of 2026-09-06. The other two of
the original 76 were deleted: Dom Pérignon P3 1993 and Quintarelli Recioto
Classico 2011 are not on the site at any vintage, so the scores had no source.

Eleven were wrong. Ordered by how far off:

        Deutz Brut Classic                90  ->  87
        Sassicaia 2020                    96  ->  94
        Ornellaia 2020                    97  ->  95
        Roagna Barbaresco Pajé 2019       98  ->  96   (the Vecchie Viti's score)
        Henri Giraud Esprit Nature        92  ->  90
        Château d'Yquem 2015              98  ->  99+
        Egly-Ouriet Brut Tradition        93  ->  92
        Jacques Selosse Version Originale 96  ->  95
        Jacques Selosse Initial Brut      94  ->  95
        Jacques Selosse Rosé Brut         95  ->  96
        Christmann Idig GG 2021           95  ->  94+

Plus four where only a `+` was missing — not a rounding difference, since the
`+` is Parker saying he expects the wine to rise on re-tasting: Troplong Mondot
2020, Philip Togni 2019, Bürklin-Wolf Gaisböhl 2017, Krug Grande Cuvée.

## Non-vintage: settled

The owner's rule (2026-09-06): **score an NV as the latest available release.**
Eleven of the thirteen needed nothing — the site carries one NV note per cuvée
with no release named. Two carry `release`:

        94   Krug — Grande Cuvée              174ème Édition  (of 158–174 reviewed, 92 to 98)
        92   De Sousa — Cuvée 3A Grand Cru    dég. 1/2016

De Sousa's later `dég. 1/2017` is scored **91+?** — Parker's own hedge, which
our score format cannot hold and a card should not print, so the 1/2016 stands.
Worth a decision if the owner disagrees.

## Open for the owner

**Walter Scott Cuvée Ruth has no vintage on our list, and the wine is
vintaged.** Parker reviews 2012 through 2022 (88, 90, 91, 92+, 96, 94) and our
94 is the 2022 exactly. Recorded as `release: "2022"`, but the real fix is the
wine's name — which vintage is in the cellar?

**Billecart's Blanc de Blancs is now labelled "Le Blanc de Blancs"**, matching
Le Réserve and Le Rosé. We list it as "Blanc de Blancs Grand Cru". The score is
the same either way (93); only the name is a question.
