## Not on the site — 2

Searched and genuinely absent, so the score we hold has no source to check it
against. Owner's call what to do with each.

        96   4850 €  Moët & Chandon — Dom Pérignon P3 1993
        98    250 €  Giuseppe Quintarelli — Recioto Classico 2011

Wine Advocate has no 1993 Dom Pérignon under any name. "P3", "Plénitude 3" and
the vintage alone all return nothing, and the Œnothèque range — what P3 was
called before the rename — is reviewed for 1990, 1995 and 1996 but not 1993.
So this is not a gap in their coverage of the era; that vintage was never
reviewed.

Quintarelli’s Recioto is the same shape. Filtering the producer to every Recioto
they have reviewed gives 2015, 2007, 2004, 1997 and 1995 — five wines, no 2011.
The nearest 98 on that list is the 2007 "A Roberto", which is a different
bottling and a different vintage; whether ours was copied from it is a guess,
so it is written down as a guess and nothing was changed.
